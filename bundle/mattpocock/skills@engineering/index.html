TLDR

Matt Pocock, a TypeScript teacher, built these skills to keep coding agents aligned with what you want and their code small and tested. A one-time setup skill records which issue tracker the repo uses and what its triage labels are called. The flow then starts with an interview: the agent asks about the change in numbered rounds, each question with its recommended answer, until no decision is left open, and writes the project's terms to a glossary and hard-to-reverse choices to architecture decision records as you go. Questions that talk can't answer go to a throwaway prototype or a background research agent. Next, the conversation becomes a spec on the tracker, then tickets: thin slices that each work end to end and list the tickets that must land first. The build step takes one ticket at a time, test-first, and ends with a review that checks the diff against your coding standards and, separately, against the spec; another skill builds a whole spec at once with parallel subagents. Finally, a retrospective proposes new checks, coding standards or agent instructions so the next run goes better.

Its skills, in the order they're used

The ones marked “Only in the bundle” need the rest of it, so they're only listed here. The others also have their own entry in the skills list.

  1. setup-matt-pocock-skills

    Only in the bundle 868K installs

    Sets up a repo for the Matt Pocock skills: issue tracker, triage labels and domain docs.

    Writes the tracker, label and domain-doc settings under docs/agents/ that the other skills read; on its own, nothing uses them.

  2. ask-matt

    Only in the bundle 591K installs

    Picks the right skill or flow from the Matt Pocock bundle for your situation.

    A map of the other skills that reads each one's SKILL.md before recommending it, so it only helps once they're installed.

  3. grill-with-docs

    Only in the bundle 1M installs

    Runs the same interview and writes ADRs and a glossary as it goes.

    Its whole SKILL.md loads grilling and domain-modeling.

  4. grill-me

    Only in the bundle 1.2M installs

    Interviews you relentlessly to sharpen a plan or design.

    Its whole SKILL.md loads grilling.

  5. grilling

    744K installs

    Stress-tests a plan, decision or idea, one round of questions at a time.

  6. domain-modeling

    672K installs

    Builds and sharpens the project's domain model, CONTEXT.md and ADRs.

  7. research

    538K installs

    Investigates a question against primary sources and saves the findings as Markdown.

  8. handoff

    842K installs

    Compacts the conversation into a handoff document for another agent.

  9. prototype

    815K installs

    Builds a throwaway prototype to answer a design question.

  10. wayfinder

    Only in the bundle 543K installs

    Plans work too big for one session as a map of decision tickets.

    Resolves its decision tickets by loading grilling, domain-modeling, research and prototype, on the tracker that setup-matt-pocock-skills configures (local Markdown files without it).

  11. to-spec

    Only in the bundle 538K installs

    Turns the conversation into a spec on the issue tracker.

    Publishes to the issue tracker, with the ready-for-agent label, that setup-matt-pocock-skills configures.

  12. to-tickets

    Only in the bundle 529K installs

    Breaks a plan into tracer-bullet tickets with blocking edges.

  13. triage

    Only in the bundle 818K installs

  14. implement

    Only in the bundle 590K installs

  15. implement-spec

  16. tdd

  17. codebase-design

  18. code-review

  19. diagnosing-bugs
